## English

**Effective date: November 3, 2026**

Catpital ("we", "us") is a paper-trading game that runs inside Discord as an Activity, together with a Discord bot. Everything you trade with in Catpital is virtual. This policy explains what information we collect when you play, how we use it, and the choices you have.

### 1. What we collect

**Your Discord account.** When you open Catpital, Discord asks you to authorize it. We then receive your Discord user ID, username, display name, avatar and language. The sign-in requests the `identify` and `guilds` permissions. We only read your basic profile: we do not fetch or store the list of servers you are in, and we never read your Discord messages.

**Your game data.** We store what you do in the game: your virtual balance, orders, positions, trade and transaction history, past lives (bankruptcy history), leaderboard results, titles, and your settings (language, sound, chart preferences, which markets appear in your top bar).

**Things you post.**

- **Chat messages ("danmaku").** These are shown to other players on the chart. They are not saved in our database. We keep only a rolling buffer of the most recent 1,000 messages per market, which may also be cleared when the server restarts.
- **Reactions** (👍 / 👎) on items in the 24-hour feed. These are stored together with your user ID.

**Bolívares exchange with Skylbot.** If you exchange currency with the Skylbot bot, we send your Discord user ID and the amount to Skylbot, and we keep a record of each exchange (amount, amount received after the fee, status and time).

**Discord server settings.** If you are a server admin and configure the bot, we store the server ID, the announcement channel, admin role IDs, the server language and which announcements are switched on.

**Channel visibility checks.** Before the bot posts an announcement about a player in a Discord channel, it asks Discord whether that player is a member of the server and can view the channel. The answer (yes or no) is kept in memory for 5 minutes and is never written to our database.

**Technical logs.** Our server keeps request logs that may include your IP address, the page or API that was requested, and the time. We use them to fix problems and prevent abuse.

**On your device.** The app saves your settings in your browser's local storage so they load quickly. Your sign-in session is kept in memory only.

### 2. How we use it

- To run the game: keep your balance and positions, fill orders, settle leaderboards and rewards.
- To show social features: leaderboards, player profiles (with the five-stat radar chart), the 24-hour feed, chat and reactions.
- To post game announcements (big wins, liquidations, bankruptcies, market news) in the app and in the Discord channels that servers have configured.
- To keep the game fair: detect cheating, bug abuse and reward farming.
- To fix bugs and keep the service running.

### 3. What other players can see

Your display name, avatar and generation ("life" number), your leaderboard rank and value, your profile statistics, your chat messages, your reactions (your avatar appears next to the feed items you reacted to), and announcements about your big wins, liquidations and bankruptcies. In Discord channels, the bot only posts announcements about you in channels that you yourself can view.

### 4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We only share it:

- **With Discord**, which runs the platform Catpital is built on.
- **With Skylbot**, and only when you use the exchange (your Discord user ID and the amount).
- **With infrastructure providers** that host or carry our traffic (for example our server host and Cloudflare), only so they can provide that service to us.
- **When the law requires it.**

The services we use for market prices (such as Yahoo Finance and Binance) do not receive any information about you.

### 5. How long we keep it

- Account and game data are kept while you play, and until you ask us to delete them.
- Chat messages are kept only in the temporary rolling buffer described above.
- Server logs are kept only as long as needed for troubleshooting and abuse prevention.

### 6. Your choices

- **Get a copy or delete your data.** Contact us (see below). Deleting your account removes your profile, wallet, history and reactions from Catpital. Announcements that were already posted in Discord channels are messages in those servers and can be removed by the server's admins.
- **Hide chat.** You can turn chat messages off in the game's settings.
- **Stop using Catpital** at any time by closing the Activity. You can also remove the app's access in Discord under User Settings → Authorized Apps.

### 7. Age

You must meet the minimum age required by Discord to use Catpital (13, or older where your local law requires).

### 8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no online service can be completely secure.

### 9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.

## 中文

**生效日期：2026 年 11 月 3 日**

Catpital（下称"我们"）是一款运行在 Discord 里的模拟交易游戏，由 Discord 活动（Activity）和一个 Discord 机器人组成，游戏里交易用的全部是虚拟货币。本政策说明你玩游戏时我们收集哪些信息、怎么使用，以及你有哪些选择。

### 1. 我们收集什么

**你的 Discord 账号信息。** 打开 Catpital 时，Discord 会请你授权，之后我们会拿到你的 Discord 用户 ID、用户名、显示名、头像和语言。登录时申请的权限是 `identify` 和 `guilds`。我们只读取基本资料：不会获取或保存你加入了哪些服务器，也从不读取你的 Discord 消息。

**你的游戏数据。** 我们保存你在游戏里的记录：虚拟余额、下单、持仓、交易和资金流水、人生履历（破产记录）、排行榜成绩、称号，以及你的设置（语言、声音、图表偏好、顶部行情栏显示哪些市场）。

**你发出的内容。**

- **弹幕。** 会显示在其他玩家的 K 线图上。弹幕不写入我们的数据库，每个市场只临时保留最近 1000 条，服务器重启时也可能被清空。
- **对 24 小时动态的 👍 / 👎。** 会和你的用户 ID 一起保存。

**和 Skylbot 兑换 Bolívares。** 如果你和 Skylbot 机器人兑换货币，我们会把你的 Discord 用户 ID 和兑换数量发给 Skylbot，并保存每一笔兑换的记录（支付数量、扣除手续费后的到账数量、状态和时间）。

**Discord 服务器设置。** 如果你是服务器管理员并设置了机器人，我们会保存服务器 ID、播报频道、管理员身份组 ID、服务器语言和各类播报的开关。

**频道可见性检查。** 机器人在频道里发和某个玩家有关的播报之前，会向 Discord 查询这个玩家是否在该服务器、能否看到该频道。查询结果（能 / 不能）只在内存里保留 5 分钟，不写入数据库。

**技术日志。** 服务器会记录请求日志，可能包括你的 IP 地址、访问的页面或接口、时间，用于排查问题和防止滥用。

**你的设备上。** 应用会把你的设置保存在浏览器本地存储里，方便下次快速加载。登录状态只保存在内存中。

### 2. 我们怎么使用

- 运行游戏：记录余额和持仓、成交订单、结算排行榜和奖励。
- 提供社交功能：排行榜、玩家主页（含五维雷达图）、24 小时动态、弹幕和点赞点踩。
- 在应用内、以及服务器设置好的 Discord 频道里发游戏播报（暴富、斩杀、破产、金融快讯）。
- 维护公平：识别作弊、利用漏洞和刷奖励。
- 修复问题，保证服务正常运行。

### 3. 其他玩家能看到什么

你的显示名、头像、第几世、排行榜名次和数值、玩家主页的统计数据、你发的弹幕、你的点赞点踩（你的头像会出现在你评价过的动态旁边），以及关于你暴富、被斩杀、破产的播报。在 Discord 频道里，机器人只会在你自己能看到的频道里发和你有关的播报。

### 4. 我们和谁分享

我们不出售你的信息，只在以下情况分享：

- **Discord**：Catpital 运行所在的平台。
- **Skylbot**：仅在你使用兑换功能时，发送你的 Discord 用户 ID 和兑换数量。
- **基础设施服务商**：为我们托管服务器或传输网络流量的服务商（例如服务器主机和 Cloudflare），仅用于为我们提供这些服务。
- **法律要求时。**

我们获取行情所用的服务（如 Yahoo Finance、Binance）不会收到任何关于你的信息。

### 5. 保留多久

- 账号和游戏数据在你游玩期间一直保留，直到你要求删除。
- 弹幕只保存在上面说的临时缓存里。
- 服务器日志只保留排查问题和防止滥用所需的时间。

### 6. 你的选择

- **获取副本或删除数据**：通过下方联系方式联系我们。删除账号会从 Catpital 移除你的资料、钱包、历史记录和点赞点踩。已经发到 Discord 频道里的播报属于那些服务器里的消息，可以由服务器管理员删除。
- **隐藏弹幕**：在游戏设置里可以关闭弹幕显示。
- **停止使用**：随时关闭活动即可。你也可以在 Discord 的「用户设置 → 已授权的应用」里取消 Catpital 的授权。

### 7. 年龄

使用 Catpital 需满足 Discord 规定的最低年龄（13 岁；所在地法律要求更高的，以当地为准）。

### 8. 安全

我们会采取合理的措施保护你的信息，但任何网络服务都无法做到绝对安全。

### 9. 变更

本政策如有修改，我们会更新本页面和顶部的日期。
